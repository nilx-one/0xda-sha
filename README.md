# 0xda-sha

a tv series about dasha, the life she had, and what remains after 2022.

2022 is not the beginning.

it is the break in the middle.

the story follows dasha before, through, and after that break — not as a symbol, not as a witness standing outside the frame, but as the person who has to keep living when the old version of her life no longer fits.

this is a story about memory, attachment, loss, survival, and the small things that refuse to disappear.

dasha is the center of it.

everything else is allowed to change.

## dasha

dasha is not defined by what happened to her.

she is defined by what she keeps carrying.

the series stays close to her perspective: the places she remembers, the people she loses, the things she cannot explain, and the ordinary details that become important only after they are gone.

there is no clean line between before and after.

there is only the person who existed before 2022, the person who came through it, and the distance between them.

## 2022

the year changes the shape of the story.

not because everything ends there.

because everything after it has to be understood differently.

the series does not treat 2022 as a single event. it is a boundary between two versions of reality — personal, physical, emotional, and historical.

what survives that boundary becomes the real subject of the story.

## the dog

there is a dog.

for now, she does not need a name.

she is not another protagonist, and not another story to follow.

she is an artifact.

a small, living fragment of the life of pasha and dasha before 24.02.2022 — something that can suddenly open a door to a world that is no longer there in the same way.

a kind of teleport.

not back in time.

just back into the feeling of being there.

## the story

this repository is the working story bible for the series.

it will hold:

- characters
- episodes
- timelines
- scenes
- locations
- recurring details
- visual and narrative references
- continuity notes

the structure can grow as the story becomes clearer.

the core stays simple:

dasha is here.

2022 happened.

life continued.

the question is what remained.
